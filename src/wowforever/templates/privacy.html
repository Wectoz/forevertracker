Privacy

WoW Forever Tracker doesn't collect anything about you. There are no accounts, cookies, ads, analytics or trackers.

What your browser keeps

  • When you last visited, so posts published since then get a “New” badge.
  • Your class, if you choose one with “Make … my class”.

Both stay in your own browser and are never sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

Nothing loads from other sites

The fonts, icons and news images are all served from this site, including the images that come from Blizzard's articles, so opening a page doesn't contact Google, Blizzard or anyone else. Your browser only goes to another site when you click a link to it, such as an article on Blizzard.com or an addon on CurseForge.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records the IP addresses of visitors in its server logs for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. The domain's DNS is handled by Cloudflare, which only answers where the site is and doesn't see the pages you visit.
